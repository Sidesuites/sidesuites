Miami 21 rules

What the Miami 21 code allows.

Miami's zoning code — Miami 21 — permits a detached ADU by right on single-family lots in T3-L, T3-O and T4–T6 through Section 3.18. T3-R lots are excluded. The framework is one of the most permissive in Florida:

  • Max ADU size: 800 sq ft, and no more than 10% of the lot (a 407 sq ft Sidesuite needs a lot of at least 4,070 sq ft).
  • Setbacks: 5 ft side, 5 ft rear, 10 ft from a side street.
  • Unit type: Miami's minimum for a one-bedroom ADU is 450 sq ft, so the 407 sq ft Sidesuite may need to be permitted as a studio.
  • Height limit: 25 ft.
  • Owner-occupancy: the ADU can be rented only while the property has a homestead exemption.
  • Parking: one off-street space for the ADU.
  • Short-term rental (under 30 days): not permitted in T3 zones for ADUs.
Miami-Dade unincorporated areas follow the County's separate ADU ordinance — similar in spirit but with different setback and lot-size requirements. If your address is in unincorporated Miami-Dade (not within a municipal limit), refer to Miami-Dade County housing. Check My Lot covers every address in Miami-Dade, city or county.

Miami rental economics

Furnished 1-bedroom long-term rents in Miami's mid-tier neighborhoods (Little Havana, Allapattah, Brownsville) sit at $1,600–$1,950/month. In high-tier neighborhoods (Coral Gables, Edgewater), 1-bed rents push past $2,300. Sidesuite is laid out as a one-bedroom, though in Miami it may be permitted as a studio. Treat these as its upper-range comparables. Sidesuite comes unfurnished, so budget for furniture or expect lower rent unfurnished. See the rental income page for the full breakdown.

Permit process

How to get an ADU permit in Miami.

  1. Pre-application zoning verification — confirm your lot is T3-L, T3-O or a higher transect (T3-R lots don't allow ADUs). Free, online via Miami's Planning Department portal.
  2. Submit building permit application — drawings, structural calcs, site plan. Sidesuite's team walks you through the site plan and permits.
  3. Review and revision — typically 4–8 weeks of city review with comments.
  4. Permit issued — construction can begin.
  5. Inspections — foundation, framing, electrical, plumbing, final.
  6. Certificate of occupancy — you can move in or rent.

The home itself is 15 weeks from order to installed. Reserving is $999, fully refundable, and charged only after your address passes.
