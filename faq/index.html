The basics

What is a Sidesuite?

What is an ADU?

An accessory dwelling unit (ADU) is a fully independent home built on the same parcel as an existing primary residence. It has its own kitchen, bathroom, and entrance. Also called backyard cottages, granny flats, in-law suites, or casitas. Sidesuite is a detached backyard home: 407 sq ft (11′ × 37′) with 1 bedroom, 1 bath and a full kitchen, in a mid-century modern design.

Can I get impact-fee waivers or property-tax exemptions?

Yes — if you voluntarily commit to renting at 100% area median income (AMI). Florida Statute 166.04151 lets cities waive impact and utility-connection fees for affordable/workforce rentals. The Live Local Act (Statute 196.1979) adds property-tax exemptions for units rented to low-income tenants. The commitment is between you and your municipality. AMI rents typically land in the $1,500–$1,900/month band across Florida metros. More on the rental income page.

Legal & permits

Is this allowed where I live?

Are ADUs legal in Florida?

Yes, in most of Florida. Florida Statute 163.31771 enables cities and counties to permit ADUs, and most major Florida municipalities have ordinances on the books. Pending state legislation (HB 313) would make ADUs by-right statewide as soon as December 2026. Full legal breakdown.

Can I check whether my lot qualifies?

Yes, for every address in Miami-Dade and Broward counties today. Check My Lot is free and instant: we check zoning, the lot and the city's rules, and rate both Sidesuite models Highly likely, Likely or Unlikely.

What permits do I need?

A building permit from your city or county. Inspections cover foundation, framing, electrical, plumbing, and final. A certificate of occupancy is issued at the end. Florida permit fees: $1,500–$6,500 depending on jurisdiction.

Will my HOA block an ADU?

Possibly. Florida HOAs can restrict ADUs through deed covenants even when the city permits them. HB 313 includes language limiting HOA discretion, but the final bill is still moving. Check your HOA's deed restrictions before applying.

Can I subdivide and sell the ADU separately?

No. Florida law does not currently allow independent fee-simple sale of an ADU on a single-family parcel. The ADU shares the underlying parcel with the primary residence.

Financing & taxes

How do I pay for it?

How do I finance a Sidesuite?

Most homeowners use a HELOC, construction-to-perm loan, or cash-out refinance. ADU-specific lenders (RenoFi, HFS Financial) underwrite against future appraised value — works if you don't have current equity. FHA 203(k) is another option. Sidesuite's own lender partners are opening soon. Compare all five paths.

Does an ADU raise my property taxes?

Yes. Typical assessment increase: 60–80% of the build cost. Your homestead exemption still applies to the primary residence; the ADU and its land share are assessed at full market rate.

How does an ADU affect my insurance?

Insurance typically rises $600–$1,000/year in Florida when you add an insured ADU.

Rental & use

Renting it out.

Can I rent it out?

Long-term rental (12+ month leases) is permitted in virtually every Florida ADU ordinance. Short-term rental (under 30 days) is regulated differently by city — some allow with a license, some restrict, some prohibit. A one-bedroom backyard home rents long-term for $1,200–$2,200/month across Florida metros. Full rent math by metro.
