City guides

ADU rules by Florida metro.

Every city has its own ADU ordinance — different max sizes, different setbacks, different owner-occupancy rules. Find yours:

Miami

One of the most ADU-friendly cities in Florida. Miami 21 permits ADUs on T3-L, T3-O and higher single-family lots (not T3-R).

Miami ADU rules →

Tampa

Tampa dropped owner-occupancy in 2022; permit timelines run 6–10 weeks.

Tampa ADU rules →

Orlando

Citywide ADU ordinance passed in 2023. Owner-occupancy required, but otherwise broadly permissive.

Orlando ADU rules →

Jacksonville

Florida's largest city by area. Consolidated city-county permitting; fast review times.

Jacksonville ADU rules →

St. Petersburg

2023 ordinance update dropped owner-occupancy. One of the best investor markets in Florida.

St. Petersburg ADU rules →

Palm Beach County

Highest rents in the state. Patchwork of city ordinances under a county Workforce Housing program.

Palm Beach ADU rules →
