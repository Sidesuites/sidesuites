What lot work costs on a typical Florida ADU

These are the lot-specific costs on any ADU build in Florida. Budget for these on top of the home and installation until your site-plan quote says otherwise:

Workforce & affordable housing

Some fees can be reduced or waived.

Florida is actively pushing housing supply, and many of the state's local incentive programs reduce or waive impact fees — which typically include water and sewer connection fees — when an ADU is rented to an affordable or workforce-housing tenant. The mechanics vary by jurisdiction, but the cost stack on your build can drop by several thousand dollars.

  • Florida Statute 166.04151 lets cities offer incentives such as reduced impact and water/sewer fees in exchange for affordable units.
  • Florida Statute 163.31771 — the ADU enabling statute — explicitly allows cities to count rental ADUs toward affordable-housing obligations when rents are capped at a percentage of area median income (AMI).
  • Local programs — Miami-Dade impact fee waivers, Hillsborough County's Affordable Housing Incentives Program (AHIP), Palm Beach County's Workforce Housing Program, and Orange County's affordable-housing impact fee deferral all reduce or waive connection fees for qualifying rentals.
What this means for your build. If you're open to renting your Sidesuite at workforce-housing rates (as your city or county defines them), the $3,000–$15,000 utility-connection line in the table above can come down significantly — sometimes to zero. Programs are local; check with your city or county housing office, and ask specifically about impact fee waivers for accessory dwelling units rented at affordable or workforce rates.

What's the cheapest ADU you can build in Florida?

A garage conversion can come in under $50,000 if the garage is already insulated and on a slab. But you're trading a garage for a small unit, and the resale and rental economics rarely beat a detached, purpose-built ADU like Sidesuite.

Are permits really that variable?

They are. Florida permit fees are calculated as a percentage of valuation plus flat impact fees, so the same ADU can carry a different permit total in Miami-Dade than in Hillsborough or Orange County. See Miami, Tampa, Orlando, and Jacksonville for city-specific numbers.
