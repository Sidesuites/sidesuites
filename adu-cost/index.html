What lot work costs on a typical Florida ADU

These are the lot-specific costs on any ADU build in Florida. Budget for these on top of the home and installation until your site-plan quote says otherwise:

  • Permit fees, paid to your city/county. Typically $1,500–$6,500 in Florida.
  • Site preparation: clearing, leveling, foundation pad. $8,000–$25,000 depending on lot.
  • Utility connections: water, sewer/septic tie-in, electrical service. $3,000–$15,000 depending on distance from existing connections.

Workforce & affordable housing

Some impact fees can be waived.

Florida lets counties and cities waive impact fees for affordable housing, but it doesn't require them to. Where a waiver or exemption exists, it isn't automatic: the unit has to be approved as affordable housing, and you sign a covenant that keeps the rent within the local limits for years. Most of these programs were written for housing developers, so ask early whether a single backyard home can use them.

  • Florida Statute 163.31801(11) lets a county, city or special district waive impact fees for affordable housing; each one decides for itself. Water and sewer connection fees fall outside that law (163.31801(12)), so don't count on the utility-connection line above coming down.
  • Florida Statute 163.31771, the ADU statute, counts each ADU a city or county allows under it toward the affordable-housing part of its comprehensive plan. More on what it requires.
  • Miami-Dade County. Ask the County's Impact Fee Section (786-315-2670) whether your unit can be exempt. The affordable-housing exemption takes a 15-year affordability covenant with the County, and the code leaves out units priced over $75,000 unless a government assistance program approves more, so ask whether a Sidesuite qualifies. It doesn't cover the school impact fee. Cities can charge their own impact fees: the City of Miami defers its fees for certified affordable units rather than waiving them. Miami-Dade's impact-fee exemptions.
  • Broward County. Start with an Affordable Housing Certification Letter from the County's Housing and Urban Planning Division (954-357-4900). A rental needs a recorded covenant of at least 20 years, signed by you and any mortgage lender. The County's own road and park impact fees have been suspended since October 1, 2024, so the waiver that still matters is the one for the school impact fee, which the School Board approves. Broward's affordable-housing fee waivers (PDF).
  • Other counties, including Hillsborough, Palm Beach and Orange, set their own rules. Check with your county's impact-fee or housing office.
What this means for your build. Budget as if you'll pay the fees. If you're open to renting at the local affordable rate for 15 to 20 years, call your county's impact-fee office before you apply for the permit and ask about an impact-fee exemption for an accessory dwelling unit rented at an affordable rate. An approved waiver comes off your impact fees, not your utility connections.
FAQ

Common ADU cost questions.

What does the $99,999 cover?

The finished home: 401 sq ft with 1 bedroom, 1 bath and a full kitchen, appliances included. Installation is billed separately: it varies by location, is estimated around $30,000 and is quoted for your lot.

What's the cheapest ADU you can build in Florida?

A garage conversion can come in under $50,000 if the garage is already insulated and on a slab. But you're trading a garage for a small unit, and the resale and rental economics rarely beat a detached, purpose-built ADU like Sidesuite.

Are permits really that variable?

They are. Florida permit fees are calculated as a percentage of valuation plus flat impact fees, so the same ADU can carry a different permit total in Miami-Dade than in Hillsborough or Orange County. See Miami, Tampa, Orlando, and Jacksonville for city-specific numbers.
